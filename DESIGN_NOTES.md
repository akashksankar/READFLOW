# ReadFlow — Design Notes ("Folio" Concept, Phase 1 & Phase 2)

## Phase 2 Rule Resolutions (Recorded per Section 1)

1. **Text animations on opening vs. "Reading text never animates"**:
   - **Decision**: Animate only the **document title, chapter openers, and the first paragraph's drop cap**, once, when a document or chapter opens. Body text is visible from frame 1 and never animates while reading or scrolling. Any scroll or tap ("Skip") snaps the sequence to its final state immediately, and Reduced Motion / Lite Mode / `"Off"` setting disables it completely.
2. **Page-flip instead of scrolling vs. "No 3D, no heavy effects"**:
   - **Decision**: Offer an optional **Paged** reading flow alongside **Scroll** (default), with three clean page-turn styles: **Slide** (default horizontal column strip drag), **Fade** (240ms crossfade), and **Flip** (a light CSS `perspective: 1400px` `rotateY(0 -> -90deg)` turn of a single page with no shadows, curl, or physics, falling back to Slide in Lite/Reduced motion).
3. **Flashcards from a page vs. "No AI features in v1; no fabricated content"**:
   - **Decision**: Cards are generated **without AI**: from the reader's own highlights and notes, from a selected passage, or from deterministic rule-based text extraction (definitions, bold terms, numerical facts with cloze `{{blank}}`, key sentences under headings, and bulleted lists). Every suggested card starts unchecked in the Card Maker so nothing is saved without explicit human review and approval, and back text always uses the document's verbatim wording.

## Screen Compositions & Rationale

- **Library (`/`)**: A quiet personal study shelf with an asymmetric Continue Reading hero, a multi-column spine-edge book cover grid (showing figure count when figure detection completes), and a stroke-drawn open book empty state.
- **Today Dashboard (`/today`)**: Composed with Folio editorial restraint rather than a SaaS card grid—a thin stroke **Goal Arc** around a large tabular Fraunces numeral for active minutes today, seven vertical swallowtail **Week Ribbons** in hand-rolled SVG, up to three **Currently Reading** covers with pace-derived time-left estimates, deterministic **Daily Challenges**, a **Personal Routine** task list, a 12-week **Calendar Heat Map**, and hairline-divided **Totals**.
- **Flashcards Deck List (`/cards`) & Study (`/cards/:deckId/study`)**: Decks appear as tactile layered stacks of colored cards (`--card-1` to `--card-8`) led by a **Saved** collection deck; the full-screen **Swipe Learning** view mounts at most 3 stacked cards with Literata typography, 3D `rotateY` flip, directional drag tilt/wash overlays (`Got it` right, `Again` left, `Save` up), explicit keyboard/button controls, and deterministic 5-box Leitner scheduling.
- **Import Sheet (Dialog / Bottom Sheet)**: A focused three-step typographic ledger (`Reading file`, `Finding text`, `Making cover`) with a real determinate `scaleX` progress bar and plain-spoken recovery actions.
- **Reader Workspace (`/read/:id`)**: Supports both **Scroll** and **Paged** (CSS multi-column) flows with a persistent 2px terracotta ribbon progress line, summonable chrome, an opening title/drop-cap sequence on Page 1, inline figure thumbnails, and a one-tap switch to **Original PDF mode** (which also includes an **Add region** tool to crop custom figures).
- **Reading Settings (Half-height Sheet / Docked Panel)**: Top **Style** preset cards (`Classic`, `Manuscript`, `Editorial`, `Study`, `Story`, `Clear`, `Plain`) with live font and drop-cap specimens, followed by hairline-grouped **Customize** controls for lazy-loaded fonts, starting-letter / drop-cap treatment (with 3:1 theme contrast validation), reading flow (`Scroll` / `Paged`), page-turn style, opening animation, inline figures, and storage usage.
- **Figures Gallery & Lightbox (`src/features/figures/`)**: A two-column masonry gallery grouped under page folios with `All / Images / Diagrams` filter chips and a full-screen zoom/pan **Lightbox** with `Go to page`, `Save image`, `Copy`, and `Add to notebook`.
- **Notebook (`/notes/:id` & Docked Panel / Tall Sheet)**: Lists highlights (with `Make card`), saved figure notes (with crop thumbnails), and ribbon bookmarks in document order under serif page folios.

## Major Component Rationales

- **`NavigationShell` (`src/features/nav/NavigationShell.tsx`)**: Renders a quiet 3-destination bottom bar on phones and a slim left rail on desktop (`Library`, `Today`, `Cards`) with a terracotta ribbon underline on the active destination and a due-cards count badge on `Cards`.
- **`ReadingStyles` & `FontPicker` (`src/features/typography/`)**: Renders horizontally scrolling style preset cards in their real fonts and drop-cap treatments, and loads variable font files lazily on selection or first specimen view.
- **`DropCapControls` (`src/features/typography/DropCapControls.tsx`)**: Drives `p.starts-section::first-letter` via CSS variables (`--dropcap-lines`, `--dropcap-color`, `--dropcap-weight`, `--dropcap-font`) and validates all candidate colors for ≥ 3:1 contrast against `--bg` in the active theme.
- **`SplitText` & `OpeningSequence` (`src/features/opening/`)**: Wraps the document title and chapter headings with a single `aria-label` while marking animated line/character spans `aria-hidden="true"` so screen readers read the title once and normally.
- **`detectFigures` & `FiguresSheet` & `Lightbox` (`src/features/figures/`)**: Inspects `page.getOperatorList()` for raster image transforms and vector path clusters, crops from a 2x page render with 6pt padding into WebP/JPEG blobs, filters watermarks and small noise, pairs `Fig./Table/Chart` captions, and displays them inline and in a gallery/lightbox.
- **`PagedReader` (`src/features/paged/PagedReader.tsx`)**: Paginates a 3-original-page sliding window using native CSS multi-column layout (`column-width`, `column-gap`, `transform: translate3d`) with `Slide`, `Fade`, and `Flip` page turns, configurable tap zones, and `(pageIndex, blockIndex, charOffset)` anchoring across font-size changes.
- **`sessionTracker` (`src/features/tracking/sessionTracker.ts`)**: Pure, UI-independent tracker (`start(docId)`, `touch()`, `recordPageView()`, `stop()`) that counts active reading seconds only when visible, focused, and interacted with in the past 60s, counts a page after ≥ 3s in view, and flushes every 15s and on `visibilitychange`/`pagehide`.
- **`GoalArc`, `WeekRibbons`, `HeatMap`, `Challenges`, `Tasks` (`src/features/today/`)**: Hand-rolled accessible SVGs and deterministic daily challenge/routine engines backed by real local activity.
- **`CardMaker`, `suggestRules`, `DeckList`, `StudyView`, `scheduler` (`src/features/cards/`)**: Rule-based non-AI card extraction (max 12 per page, unchecked by default), 5-box Leitner scheduler, and 60fps 3-card stack study interface.
